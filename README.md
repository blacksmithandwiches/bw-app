# b&w — Blacksmith & Witches (app)

The social and community app of **Blacksmith & Witches** — search at the door, people inside.

**Live:** https://app.blacksmithandwitches.com

A single-page app for the whole house:

- **Search** — one box across the books, films, the Catalogue, the Reading Room, the free Resistance Kit and the BlacksmithPay wallet demo
- **BookStore** — 'Paul of Teraura' and the Blacksmith & Witches Classics
- **Entertainment** — the animated series
- **News** — house news
- **Social Network** — the community door (Kepler's Board)
- **House Dashboard** — every house site in one place

House colours: white, flag blue `#005BBB`, yellow `#FFD500`. The three house marks — Shield, Emblem, Monogram — appear on the page in their roles.

By Dima Grossman · Blacksmith & Witches
